# Tarot content provenance

The English orientation and topic keywords in meanings.source.json are adapted from [Tarotoo Tarot Dataset](https://github.com/Tarotoo-com/tarotoo-tarot-dataset), data/cards.json, retrieved 2026-10-12. The source describes AI-assisted drafting with human review. No AI service is called by this application. Yes/no, zodiac/planet correspondences and predictive claims are not used.

Chinese explanatory copy, compositional rules and all SVG illustrations are original project editorial work. Illustrations are symbolic scenes inspired by the broad Waite tradition, not reproductions of the Rider–Waite–Smith deck. V1 saved readings retain their original content version; V2 reports are stored snapshots.
