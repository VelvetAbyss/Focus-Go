The science of starting

It's not laziness.
It's start friction.

Research on procrastination keeps landing on the same point: people don't lack intention — they get stuck at the exact moment of beginning. Scientists call it the intention–action gap. We call it start friction, and it's the one thing this product is built to lower.

0%of adults are chronic procrastinators
0%of students say procrastination is habitual for them
0Mpeople worldwide live with an anxiety disorder (WHO, 2019)
r = .34the measured link between procrastination and anxiety — 88 studies, 63,323 people

Right between "friction rises" and "you put it off" — the only point research says is both measurable and changeable in the moment. A visible timer, a smallest-first-step task field, and nothing to configure: the loop never gets its first win.
