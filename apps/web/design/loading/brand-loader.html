01 · Concept

Aperture — concentric rings around a warm amber point. A literal rendering of focus: the outer world holds still, the inner ring breathes, the center burns steady. The middle ring rotates at 36s — slow enough that motion registers subconsciously, never as spinning.

Alt / Meridian — a solitary sundial arc sweeping the hour. Directional, carries the go. Consider for error-recovery or longer-wait surfaces.
